# Screenshot copy and visual direction

## Captions

Use two visible lines when that matches the approved layout. Make the first line the hook
and the second the payoff. Prefer a short imperative outcome, normally three words and
no more than five in English, without terminal punctuation or repeated headline words.
Adapt these limits when another language needs a natural phrase.

Store captions with stable keys:

```json
{
  "01": ["First line", "Second line"],
  "02": ["First line", "Second line"],
  "03": ["First line", "Second line"]
}
```

Keep keys and array shapes aligned across locales. Transcreate the shared intent using
established UI terminology, native advertising language, natural line breaks, and the
correct direction for RTL scripts. Shorten the idea before shrinking text excessively.

## Direction presentation

For each credible direction include:

- a name and one-sentence product-specific concept
- exact background palette
- motif and its connection to the product
- typography and locale implications
- device framing and any faithful real-UI breakout
- support for the screenshot story
- principal tradeoff

Include conservative, expressive, and strategically different options when all three are
credible. Avoid isolated color choices and generic decoration.
