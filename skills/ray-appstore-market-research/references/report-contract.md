# Market research report contract

Organize the report around the decision:

1. scope, defaults, evidence sources, and dates
2. executive summary
3. competitor landscape and positioning differences
4. keyword demand and competitive supply
5. review themes with sample provenance
6. customer segments and jobs to be done
7. opportunity map and prioritization
8. recommendations, unknowns, and next evidence to collect

For each important conclusion, distinguish observed evidence from inference. Preserve
tool-native numbers and scales. Use tables where they improve comparison, but explain
the decision rather than outputting raw tables alone.
