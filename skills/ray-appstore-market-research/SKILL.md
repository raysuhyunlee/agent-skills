# App Store Market Research

Produce an evidence-backed market report rather than a list of results.

## 1. Resolve the scope

Determine the seed keyword and variants, platform, store country, result language,
sample sizes, and whether a product is being compared. Infer missing details from the
request and project; otherwise state reasonable defaults.

Keep rankings, ratings, prices, reviews, and keyword metrics attached to their store,
platform, language, country, and collection date. Do not merge different native scales
into one value.

## 2. Discover and shortlist

Read [references/tool-routing.md](references/tool-routing.md) before using store-research
tools or changing tracked Astro state.

Use independent discovery sources when available, deduplicate by stable store ID, and
note meaningful disagreements. Capture direct substitutes, category leaders, focused
niche products, and adjacent alternatives.

Shortlist a diverse set of relevant competitors based on job fit, rank, rating evidence,
positioning, and useful review coverage. Default to 5–8 for deep analysis, but adjust to
the market rather than filling a quota.

## 3. Analyze demand and supply

Build keyword candidates from category language, problems, outcomes, workflows,
audiences, situations, synonyms, competitor positioning, and review vocabulary.

For each serious term report native metrics and assess:

- relevance to the product promise
- search intent
- demand or popularity
- difficulty or competitive supply
- competitor coverage
- head-term or long-tail role

Do not manufacture a combined score unless its formula and normalization are explicit.
Prefer a qualitative priority with a short rationale.

## 4. Analyze reviews

Use recent reviews for current problems and helpfulness-ranked reviews when durable
themes need confirmation. Record sample size, store, language, sort order, and date.

Look for usage triggers, valued outcomes, must-have features, repeated friction, feature
requests, monetization reactions, trust and localization concerns, and switching
behavior. Classify findings as recurring themes, emerging signals, or anecdotes.

Quote only raw text returned by a review tool, keep quotations short, and identify the
app and rating. Reviews are self-selected evidence, not market share.

## 5. Infer customers and opportunities

Derive segments from observed jobs, contexts, constraints, and language rather than
demographics alone. Describe their trigger, job, outcome, workaround, pain, decision
criteria, monetization signal, evidence, and confidence.

Label claims:

- **Observed:** directly supported by metadata, rankings, metrics, or reviews
- **Inferred:** reasoned interpretation of observed evidence
- **Unknown:** important question the data cannot answer

Prioritize opportunities where demand overlaps repeated unmet need and weak execution.
Consider frequency, severity, demand, supply, differentiation, feasibility, and
monetization without treating a popular keyword as product validation by itself.

## 6. Cross-check and report

Before concluding, verify that competitors fit the seed job, product claims have
evidence, review themes are not generalized from anecdotes, metrics retain provenance,
and recommendations distinguish research evidence from judgment.

Use the report contract in [references/report-contract.md](references/report-contract.md).
Lead with decisions and opportunities, cite raw evidence near claims, disclose defaults
and unavailable data, and avoid false precision.
